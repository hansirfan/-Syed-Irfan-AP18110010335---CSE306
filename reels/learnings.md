## Hypotheses to test
- H1: A hook line stating why the pairing is surprising beats a plain "A x B" title.
- H2: 8-bar transitions (payoff by ~10s) hold viewers better than 16-bar ones (~19s).
- H3: Pairings where both songs are widely known get more shares than club-only tracks.
- H4: Showing BPM and key in frame draws DJ-minded followers (comments, saves).
