## India Compensation Negotiation Playbook

### CTC Context
- **Current CTC: 19 LPA** (at Whatfix, serving notice)
- **Target: 30–50 LPA** — this is a 58%+ hike, achievable given ARR impact and enterprise depth
- **Floor: 28 LPA fixed** — do not accept below this under any framing
- **Available: Immediate to 30 days** — use this as leverage ("I can join quickly")
- Do NOT reveal 19 LPA unless legally required or directly asked. Say: "I'm targeting 30–35 LPA based on market and my impact" — not "I'm currently at 19 and want 30"

### When to Counter
- Always counter if verbal offer is below 35 LPA
- Counter window: 24–48 hours after receiving written offer
- Never negotiate via WhatsApp — always email or call

### What to Say
"Thank you for the offer. I'm genuinely excited about the role and team. Based on my 4 years of direct impact — including $480K ARR expansion and 100% client retention across enterprise accounts — and current market rates for this role, I was targeting 30–35 LPA. Is there flexibility to get closer to that range?"

### Typical Negotiation Room by Company Stage
| Stage | Fixed | Variable | ESOPs | Typical Room |
|-------|-------|----------|-------|--------------|
| Series B (50–200 people) | 25–38 LPA | 10–20% | Yes (vest 4yr) | 15–25% counter room |
| Series C/D (200–1000) | 30–50 LPA | 10–15% | Yes | 10–15% |
| Enterprise/MNC | 28–45 LPA | 15–25% | Rarely | 5–10%, push variable |

### ESOPs in India (2026)
- Only meaningful at Series A–C with strong investor backing (Sequoia, Tiger, Accel India)
- Ask: cliff period (typically 1 year), exercise price, secondary liquidity options
- Do not take a 20% pay cut for ESOPs at a Series A — risk too high

### What Syed Should Not Accept
- Below 28 LPA fixed regardless of variable/ESOPs
- Full on-site mandatory outside Bengaluru without significant premium
- "We'll review in 6 months" without a written performance milestone attached
