## On-the-Day Reminders

1. **Lead with problems, not features.** Don't pitch your solutions. Ask about their biggest adoption challenges first.
2. **Use metrics everywhere.** "65-75% adoption in 90 days" > "We achieved good adoption."
3. **Show you listen.** In every answer, reference something from the interviewer's last comment.
4. **Multi-stakeholder is your superpower.** Use this word in every interview. Claravine's entire problem is multi-stakeholder.
5. **Regulated industries matter.** Insurance, pharma, financial services—your sweet spot, Claravine's market.
6. **Playbooks scale.** Every time you mention something that worked once, say "We codified this, scaled it to X accounts, drove Y outcome."
7. **End on expansion.** Most CS roles focus on retention. You should mention expansion in almost every answer.

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ "I have sales experience too" (Claravine doesn't want CS people who want to be sales)
- ❌ "I worked at a different company, the process is..." (Lead with customer focus, not process)
- ❌ "I can build a team from scratch" (You can, but lead with adopting the *existing* team first)
- ❌ "I'll reduce churn to zero" (Unrealistic; shift to "drive expansion + retention")
- ❌ Focusing on features instead of outcomes (Claravine cares about customer adoption outcomes, not software features)
